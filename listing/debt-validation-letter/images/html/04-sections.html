What the template covers

  • Your Information — your name, street address, optional email and phone
  • The Debt Collector — company name and mailing address
  • Date and Reference — the date you send it, the name on the account, the collector's reference number
  • Statement of Dispute — Option A dispute in full, or Option B dispute part of it and describe which part
  • Request for Verification — the request itself, citing the federal statute, with an optional note to remove that citation
  • What I Am Asking You to Provide — the items you are asking the collector to send you
  • Request to Pause Collection Activity — a request, written into the letter, that collection activity pause while verification is provided
  • Response Period — a blank where you enter a number of days for the reply
  • Enclosures — optional
  • Closing and Signature — a single signature block (a letter from one sender, not a two-party agreement)
Plus a one-page “How to use this template” cover and a full disclaimer at the end. Section 4 sets two options; you keep the one you want and delete the other.
