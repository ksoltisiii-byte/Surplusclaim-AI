## Deliberate omissions — the claims discipline for this product

- **No claim that the letter removes, deletes, corrects, or clears anything.** Section 7 is described as "your request, written into the letter" — the letter's own ask is not quoted as a benefit, because "delete the item" is on the banned list and reads as a promised outcome in marketing.
- **No claim that a bureau must reinvestigate, must respond, or must do so within any time.** The response period is described only as "a blank where you enter a number of days".
- **No claim about credit scores** in either direction.
- **No credit-repair framing of any kind** — no "repair", "fix", "clean", "boost", "improve".
- **No advice on what to dispute or which option to choose.** Options A–D are listed as options, equally, with no ranking or example theory.
- **No recommendation of a bureau.** The three are named factually inside the blank, and the copy says explicitly that nothing recommends one.
- **No reviews, ratings, buyer counts, or results** — there are no customers yet.
- **No urgency, no comparison to competitors, no state-specific suitability claim.**
